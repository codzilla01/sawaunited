# Withdrawn research draft — not a source of verified facts

This earlier working draft is withdrawn and superseded. It contained claims about legal relationships, operating figures, customers, partnerships, certifications, locations, products, services, and contact details that were not independently substantiated for publication.

Do not rely on or republish its former contents. The current portfolio content is maintained in `scripts/portfolio_builder/sawa_data.py`; it is limited to sector names and website links listed in the site materials, with explicit notes that these do not verify ownership, legal status, current operations, availability, or contact validity. Sector visuals are illustrative, not evidence of completed projects.

See `docs/technical-audit-and-redesign-plan.md` for the implementation scope and verification limitations. This working document is not included in the deployment package.
